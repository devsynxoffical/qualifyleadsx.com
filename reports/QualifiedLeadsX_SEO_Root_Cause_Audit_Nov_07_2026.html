QualifiedLeadsX: SEO Root-Cause Audit and Fixes

Saturday, November 7, 2026 · Property: https://www.qualifiedleadsx.com/ · Data: Google Search Console API (performance 1 Aug to 7 Nov 2026, URL Inspection of all 115 sitemap URLs) and a crawl of the production build
  • The site is not a 'month-old site with 200 indexed pages and no visibility'. Search Console shows Google only began showing it on 22 October 2026: 111 impressions and 5 clicks in total, about 7 to 11 a day, across 26 pages.
  • Only 69 of the 115 sitemap URLs are indexed. 41 have never been crawled: 26 are 'Discovered - not indexed' and 15 are unknown to Google. 45 of the 69 indexed pages have never received an impression.
  • Where the site does appear, it is for brand searches (homepage position 1.9) or broad commercial terms at positions 60 to 90, e.g. 'high ticket lead generation' at 75. That pattern is what a new, low-authority domain competing for head terms looks like.
  • Code-level problems made this worse and are now fixed: calculator pages declared the homepage as canonical, 10 case studies were exact duplicates, the sitemap listed noindex pages with fake 'today' dates, 41 pages were effectively undiscoverable, and several pages competed for the same keywords.
  • The biggest remaining limits are external: domain age, backlinks/authority, and ~60 templated pages with ~200-240 unique words each. These need time and off-site work, not more code.

6. AEO and GEO improvements

  • Entity consistency: one brand name (QualifiedLeadsX) across titles and site metadata, matching the Organization schema, llms.txt and sameAs profiles.
  • llms.txt now maps every service, the industry hub, tools, benchmarks, comparisons and booking with one-line factual descriptions, so AI crawlers can retrieve the right page.
  • Accurate case-study titles and descriptions built from the actual client results (no mislabelled categories), which matters for answer engines that quote specifics.
  • Existing AEO assets kept and now reachable: question-led answer boxes, FAQ sections with FAQPage schema, the 29-term glossary (DefinedTermSet), speakable selectors and Person/Organization graph.
  • robots.txt already allows Googlebot, Bingbot and AI crawlers (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended). No change needed.
  • No guarantees: none of this forces citations. It removes ambiguity so engines can match the brand and pages to the right questions.
