## 1. Core Plan Rules for Every Single Page Touched
* **Title:** Under 60 characters, target keyword first, brand once or not at all.
* **Meta Description:** Under 155 characters, must include the keyword and at least one concrete number.
* **Headings:** Exactly ONE H1 per page matching the title keyword (logos/taglines are never H1s).
* **Direct Answer (AEO Block):** Keyword in the first 100 words, followed by a 40 to 60-word concise direct answer paragraph.
* **FAQ Block & Schema:** 3 to 6 autocomplete questions, 40 to 70-word concise answers, marked up with `FAQPage` JSON-LD schema.
* **Internal Linking:** Minimum 3 internal links into every new page the day it goes live, and 3 links out.
* **Schema Standards:** `Organization` or `BlogPosting` with author/dates, plus `BreadcrumbList`.
* **Indexing Workflow:** Immediate Search Console indexing request + IndexNow submission.
