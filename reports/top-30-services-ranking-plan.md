## What the data says
- **Only 1 of 30 services is close to page 1** (the mastermind, at 19). None of the 30 is on page 1 yet.
- **7 service pages are not in Google at all:** the 3 new pages, CRM automation, financial advisors, home improvement and law firms. All 7 were submitted to Google and Bing on 19 Nov.
- **9 pages are indexed but get no impressions.** Google knows them but ranks them below about 100. Changing the text on these pages won't fix that: they need links and reviews from other sites.
- **The site's results are its advantage** (8,810 MVA leads, 5,246 roofing leads, 37 mortgage closings). That's why Tier A is where rankings are most realistic first.
