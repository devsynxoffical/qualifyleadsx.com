# Pricing: Meta Ads Management and Full-Service Growth

QualifiedLeadsX has two plans: Meta Ads Management, and Full-Service Growth, which covers ads, funnel, follow-up and booking. Both are priced as a custom quote after a free 30-minute strategy call, because the work depends on your offer, market and current funnel. This page explains what each plan includes, what changes the price, and how the 90-day written guarantee works.

Everything in Meta Ads Management, plus:

1. **Free strategy call (30 minutes).** We review your offer, price point, close rate and current lead flow.
2. **Written proposal.** You get the plan, the scope, the milestones for the 90-day guarantee, and the fee.
