# Growth plan: impressions, clicks and positions (22 Nov 2026)

Source: Google Search Console, 25 Oct – 21 Nov 2026 (28 days), qualifiedleadsx.com.

## Where we are

- **28 days:** 800 impressions and 8 clicks.
- **Trend:** impressions per day went from about 10 (6–7 Nov) to about 100 (18–19 Nov).
- **The main problem is position, not indexing.** 109 of 119 pages are indexed. But most impressions come from positions 50–90 (pages 5–9 of Google), where nobody clicks.
  - "customer acquisition agency": 57 impressions at position 70–73 (on /for/agencies)
  - "qualified lead generation services": 28 impressions at position 70–86, split across 3 pages
  - "qualified b2b leads": 15 impressions at position 68 (on /)
- **Only a few pages are on page 1 or 2:**

  | Page | Position | Impressions | Clicks |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | /about | 2.5 | 35 | 1 |
  | /benchmarks/close-rate | 3.5 | 29 | 1 |
  | /benchmarks | 6.3 | 13 | 0 |

  | /case-studies | 12.8 | 12 | 0 |

- **What this means:** Google already shows the site for the right topics. It doesn't yet trust the site enough to rank it high. Trust comes mostly from links and mentions on other sites, plus clear, single pages for each topic.

### 6. New content (only where demand is proven)

- 2 new articles a month, only for searches that already show impressions or that appear in your Keyword Planner.
  - Each is written from real case-study numbers.
  - First candidates: "cost per qualified meeting" (11 impressions), "lead scoring template" (15), "hvac meta ads" (14).
- These first strengthen existing pages (tool, template, HVAC article). A new page is only made where no page answers the question.

- **Sections 2–5 can be done by us.** Without section 1 (links and reviews), expect the lower end of these ranges.
- **Rankings move slowly.** Google takes 2–6 weeks to react to page changes, and links take longer.

## How we measure

- **Every 12 hours:** rank monitor plus a GSC daily snapshot (reports/rank-monitor-latest.md, docs/seo-log.md).
- **Every change:** a row in reports/recovery-tracker.csv with the before numbers, the validation method and a check date.
- **Weekly:** a short report on impressions, clicks, pages in the top 10 and the top movers.
