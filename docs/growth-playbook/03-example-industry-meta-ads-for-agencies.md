# Meta Ads for Agencies

Most agencies get clients from referrals, and growth stops when the referrals do. Meta ads can replace that with a steady flow of booked calls from business owners who already want help. Running ads for your own agency is different from running them for clients, though. The buyer is skeptical, the offer is intangible, and one closed retainer has to pay for weeks of spend. Below is how we run Meta ads for agencies, and what the results look like.

## Why agencies need Meta ads for their own growth

**Referrals do not scale.** A referral pipeline grows with your network, not with your capacity. When you hire, you need new clients on a schedule. Referrals arrive when they arrive.

**Your buyers are on Facebook and Instagram.** The owners of the businesses you serve, such as coaches, clinics, ecommerce brands and local service firms, use Meta every day. Broad targeting with strong creative finds them more cheaply than LinkedIn for most agency offers. In 2026 we see tier-1 CPMs average about $24.50 outside Q4 ([Meta ads CPM benchmarks](/benchmarks/meta-ads-cpm)).

**One retainer pays for a lot of testing.** At a $3,000 to $5,000 monthly retainer, a qualified booked call usually costs $95 to $160 in our accounts ([cost per booked call benchmarks](/benchmarks/cost-per-booked-call)). A few closed clients a month cover the ad spend many times over.

**Proof is the product.** Agencies sell results, and ads let you show them: case-study creative, client video testimonials and live ad account walkthroughs.

## What we do for agencies

1. **Offer and niche audit.** We check which client niche and offer your case studies can prove. "We help med spa agencies book consults" converts. "Full-service digital marketing" does not.
2. **Proof-led creative.** We make 40 to 100+ creatives: founder-to-camera videos, client results, screen recordings of real dashboards and objection-handling ads. Most agency ads fail on creative, not targeting.
3. **Qualifying funnel.** We build a 3 to 5 page funnel with a short VSL, a case-study page and an application form. The form asks about revenue, budget and decision-maker status, so tire-kickers do not reach your calendar.
4. **Speed to lead and setting.** New applicants get a reply in under 5 minutes by SMS, email and WhatsApp. Qualified applicants are booked straight into your calendar ([appointment setting for agencies](/appointment-setting-for-agencies)).
5. **Weekly optimization.** We track cost per booked call, show rate and close rate. Then we change angles, audiences and follow-up, not just budgets.

## Opportunities agencies should target

- **Niche-specific angles.** "Patient acquisition for med spas" beats "Facebook ads services".
- **Result-led hooks.** Use a specific number from a real client, such as "$639K in two months for a med spa agency", with permission.
- **Low-ticket entry offers.** A paid audit, workshop or template can pay back the ad spend before the retainer sale. See our [low-ticket offer case studies](/case-studies/digital-media-agency-157k).
- **Retargeting with proof.** Show case-study videos to people who watched 50% of your VSL or started the application.

## Mistakes agencies make with their own Meta ads

1. **Selling services instead of outcomes.** "SEO, ads and web design" gives the buyer nothing to picture. A niche and a result do.
2. **Sending cold traffic to a calendar.** Without an application step, close rates on unvetted bookings fall to 9% to 14% ([close rate benchmarks](/benchmarks/close-rate)).
3. **Slow follow-up.** An application left for a day is usually lost. Reply in under 5 minutes.
4. **Too few creatives.** Two or three ads burn out within weeks. Agencies need a steady creative pipeline, just like their clients.
5. **Judging on cost per lead.** Cheap leads that never book cost more than expensive leads that close. Track cost per booked call and cost per client.
