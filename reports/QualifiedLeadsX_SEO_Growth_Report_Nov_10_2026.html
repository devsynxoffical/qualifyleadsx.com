Findings

  • Cannibalisation on the head term: “high ticket lead generation” shows three URLs (/high-ticket-lead-generation at 55, /blog at 75, a strategies post at 93). The service page was the thinnest of the three relative to intent; it is now the most complete answer.
  • Thin core pages: /speed-to-lead and /lead-generation-agency get impressions at positions 87–91 with under 550 unique words, much of it the same 8-step block shared across service pages.
  • Proof gap on industry pages: law firms, home improvement and med spas had no matching case study although real results existed in the data as noindexed caption pages.
  • Competitor SERPs: “high ticket lead generation agency” is led by directories (Clutch, DesignRush, SyncGTM, Salesforge) and outbound agencies; “facebook ads budget calculator” is led by tool pages (Elementor, MeasureU, AdManage). Tools rank for tool queries, which is why the calculator was built rather than another article.
